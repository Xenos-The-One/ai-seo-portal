## Frequently Asked Questions

### Getting Started

**Q: How do I access the portal for the first time?**

Your agency will send you a welcome email containing your unique portal URL and authentication instructions. Click the URL, select your preferred sign-in method (typically Google, Microsoft, or email), and complete the authentication process. Once authenticated, you'll be automatically directed to your dashboard.

**Q: What browsers are supported?**

The portal works best on the latest versions of Google Chrome, Mozilla Firefox, Safari, and Microsoft Edge. For optimal performance, ensure your browser is updated to the most recent version. Internet Explorer is not supported.

**Q: Can I access the portal on my mobile device?**

Yes, the portal is fully responsive and works on smartphones and tablets. However, for the best content review experience, we recommend using a desktop or laptop computer with a larger screen.

**Q: I forgot my login credentials. What should I do?**

If you're using Google or Microsoft authentication, use your standard account recovery process for those services. If you're using email-based authentication, simply enter your email address on the login page and you'll receive a new login link. If you continue to experience issues, contact your agency for assistance.

### Content Review and Approval

**Q: How will I know when content is ready for review?**

You'll receive an email notification when content status changes to "In Progress," indicating it's ready for your review. Additionally, you can check your dashboard at any time to see all content and their current statuses.

**Q: How long do I have to review content?**

While there's no strict deadline, we recommend reviewing content within 2-3 business days of receiving the notification. This helps maintain a consistent publishing schedule and ensures timely SEO impact. If you need more time, communicate this to your agency so they can adjust expectations.

**Q: Can I edit content directly, or do I need to request changes?**

You can do both. The portal includes a rich text editor in the Edit tab where you can make direct changes to the content. For minor edits (fixing a typo, adjusting a sentence), feel free to edit directly. For major revisions or structural changes, we recommend adding comments with your feedback so your agency can make the changes while maintaining proper formatting and SEO optimization.

**Q: What if I'm not happy with the content?**

If content doesn't meet your expectations, provide specific feedback in the Comments section explaining what needs to be changed. Your agency will revise the content based on your feedback and resubmit it for your review. This process can repeat as many times as necessary until you're completely satisfied.

**Q: Can I approve content with conditions?**

Yes, but be clear about your conditions. If the change is minor (such as "approved pending one small typo fix"), you can mark it as "Approved" and add a comment explaining the condition. If the change is substantial, keep the status as "In Progress" and clearly explain what needs to be done before approval.

**Q: What happens after I approve content?**

Once you approve content, your agency receives an immediate notification. They will then schedule the content for publication according to your content calendar, or publish it immediately if that's your preference. You'll typically receive a confirmation when the content goes live on your website.

**Q: Can I un-approve content after I've approved it?**

If you need to revoke approval before the content has been published, contact your agency immediately. They can change the status back to "In Progress" and make any necessary revisions. Once content is published to your website, changes would need to be made directly on your site.

### Content Creation and Strategy

**Q: How does the AI generate content?**

The AI uses advanced language models trained on vast amounts of text data. When your agency inputs your content brief (including topic, keywords, and requirements), the AI analyzes the information, conducts research on the topic, and generates a comprehensive article structured for readability and SEO performance. Your agency then reviews and refines the AI output before presenting it to you for approval.

**Q: Can I request specific topics?**

Absolutely. You can submit content briefs through the portal specifying exactly what topics you want covered. Include as much detail as possible—target keywords, specific points to address, audience information, and desired tone. The more detailed your brief, the better the resulting content will match your vision.

**Q: How do you ensure content is original and not plagiarized?**

All content goes through plagiarism checking as part of the quality assurance process before reaching you. The AI generates original content based on its training, and your agency verifies originality before submission. If any concerns arise, your agency addresses them immediately.

**Q: Can the AI write in different tones or styles?**

Yes, the AI can adapt to various tones and styles including professional, casual, technical, conversational, authoritative, and more. Specify your desired tone in your content briefs, and your agency will configure the AI generation parameters accordingly. Over time, as you provide feedback, the AI learns your preferred style and applies it automatically.

**Q: How long are the blog posts?**

Blog post length varies based on your requirements and the topic's complexity. Typical posts range from 1,000 to 2,500 words. You can specify desired length in your content briefs. Longer, more comprehensive posts (2,000+ words) tend to perform better for SEO, but shorter posts can be effective for certain topics or audiences.

**Q: Can I set up recurring content?**

Yes, your agency can establish recurring content plans that automatically generate content on a schedule you define (daily, weekly, biweekly, or monthly). These plans include topic generation, so the AI continuously creates fresh content without requiring individual briefs for each piece. You still review and approve each piece before publication.

### Features and Functionality

**Q: What is the command palette (Ctrl+K)?**

The command palette is a quick search feature accessible by pressing Ctrl+K (Windows) or Cmd+K (Mac). It allows you to search for clients, content, templates, and pages without navigating through menus. Simply type what you're looking for and select from the results.

**Q: Can I download content for offline review?**

Yes, use the Export function to download content in multiple formats including Microsoft Word (.docx), PDF, Markdown (.md), or plain text (.txt). This allows you to review content offline, share it with team members, or keep local copies for your records.

**Q: How do I view analytics for published content?**

Navigate to the Analytics section from the main menu. You'll see performance metrics for all published content, including page views, engagement rates, time on page, and conversions. You can view overall trends or drill down into individual pieces of content to see detailed performance data.

**Q: What is the white-labeled client portal?**

The white-labeled client portal is a version of the portal that displays your agency's branding (logo, colors) rather than generic branding. This creates a seamless experience where the portal feels like a natural extension of your agency's services. All functionality remains the same.

**Q: Can multiple people from my team access the portal?**

This depends on your agreement with your agency. Many agencies offer multi-user access, allowing different team members to review and approve content. Contact your agency to discuss adding additional users to your account.

**Q: Is there a mobile app?**

Currently, there is no dedicated mobile app, but the portal is fully responsive and works well on mobile browsers. You can access all features from your smartphone or tablet by navigating to the portal URL in your mobile browser.

### Technical Questions

**Q: Is my data secure?**

Yes, the portal uses industry-standard security measures including HTTPS encryption for all data transmission, secure authentication protocols, and role-based access controls. Your content is only visible to you and your agency team. The platform is regularly updated with security patches and follows best practices for data protection.

**Q: What happens if the portal is down?**

The portal is hosted on reliable infrastructure with high uptime guarantees. In the rare event of downtime, your agency will be notified immediately and will communicate with you about the status and expected resolution time. Your data is backed up regularly, so there's no risk of data loss.

**Q: Can I integrate the portal with my website's CMS?**

Your agency can configure webhook integrations that allow approved content to be automatically published to your website's CMS (such as WordPress, Ghost, or Webflow). This eliminates the need for manual copying and pasting. Ask your agency about setting up publishing integrations for your specific platform.

**Q: Why am I seeing old error messages in the browser console?**

Occasionally, browser caching can cause old error messages to persist in the developer console even after issues have been resolved. These cached errors don't affect functionality. Try clearing your browser cache (Ctrl+Shift+Delete) and refreshing the page. If you continue to experience actual functional issues, contact your agency.

**Q: The portal is loading slowly. What can I do?**

Slow loading can be caused by several factors. First, check your internet connection speed. Next, try clearing your browser cache and cookies. Close unnecessary browser tabs that might be consuming resources. If the issue persists, try accessing the portal from a different browser or device. If problems continue, contact your agency as there may be a server-side issue.

### Best Practices

**Q: How can I get the best results from the AI content generation?**

Provide detailed content briefs with specific information about your topic, target audience, desired tone, and key points to cover. Include examples of content styles you admire. The more context you provide, the better the AI can generate content that matches your expectations. Additionally, provide clear, specific feedback on initial content so the system learns your preferences over time.

**Q: How often should I check the portal?**

We recommend checking the portal at least 1-2 times per week to stay on top of content ready for review. Setting up a regular schedule (such as every Monday and Thursday) helps ensure timely reviews and maintains a consistent publishing cadence.

**Q: Should I approve content even if it's not perfect?**

Only approve content when you're genuinely satisfied with it. The portal is designed to facilitate revisions, so don't feel pressured to approve content that needs changes. That said, remember that no content is ever absolutely perfect—if the content is high-quality and any remaining concerns are extremely minor, approval may be appropriate.

**Q: How detailed should my feedback be?**

The more detailed, the better. Instead of saying "this section needs work," explain specifically what's wrong and what you'd prefer. For example: "The pricing section should mention our new enterprise tier and include the starting price of $999/month." Specific feedback leads to faster, more accurate revisions.

**Q: Can I use content from the portal elsewhere?**

The content created for you is yours to use as you see fit. You can publish it on your website, repurpose it for social media, include it in newsletters, or use it in any other way that benefits your business. Just ensure you're following any agreements you have with your agency regarding content ownership and usage rights.

### Troubleshooting

**Q: I can't log in. What should I try?**

First, ensure you're using the correct portal URL provided by your agency. Verify you're using the correct authentication method (Google, Microsoft, or email). Clear your browser cache and cookies, then try again. Ensure your browser is up to date. If using email authentication, check your spam folder for the login link. If problems persist, contact your agency for assistance.

**Q: Content isn't displaying correctly. What should I do?**

Try refreshing the page (F5 or Ctrl+R). If that doesn't work, clear your browser cache and reload. Ensure you're using a supported browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge - latest versions). Try accessing the portal from a different browser or device to determine if it's a browser-specific issue. If the problem continues, take a screenshot and contact your agency with details about what you're seeing.

**Q: I submitted feedback but haven't heard back. How long should I wait?**

Your agency typically responds to feedback within 1-2 business days. If you haven't heard back within this timeframe, check your email spam folder for notifications. You can also check the content in the portal to see if the status has changed or if your agency has added comments in response. If you still haven't received a response after 3 business days, contact your agency directly.

**Q: The Export function isn't working. What can I try?**

Ensure your browser isn't blocking pop-ups or downloads from the portal. Check your browser's download settings and ensure downloads are enabled. Try exporting in a different format (if Word isn't working, try PDF). Clear your browser cache and try again. If the issue persists, contact your agency—they may be able to export and send the content to you directly.

**Q: I accidentally approved content I didn't mean to approve. Can I undo it?**

If the content hasn't been published yet, contact your agency immediately. They can revert the status back to "In Progress" and hold off on publication. If the content has already been published, you'll need to work with your agency to make any necessary changes directly on your website.

## Glossary of Terms

**AI (Artificial Intelligence)**: The technology that powers content generation in the portal, using advanced language models to create blog posts and articles.

**Content Brief**: A document or form you submit specifying the requirements for a new piece of content, including topic, keywords, audience, and tone.

**Content Card**: The visual representation of a piece of content on your dashboard, showing the title, featured image, status, and other key information.

**Dashboard**: The main page of the portal where all your content is displayed in an organized, browsable format.

**Featured Image**: The AI-generated image that accompanies a blog post, displayed prominently at the top of the article.

**Preview Tab**: The view that displays content exactly as it will appear when published, allowing you to review the final product.

**Edit Tab**: The view that provides a rich text editor for making direct changes to content.

**Status**: The current stage of content in the workflow (Draft, In Progress, or Approved).

**White-Labeling**: The customization of the portal with your agency's branding (logo, colors) to create a seamless branded experience.

**Webhook**: An automated integration that allows approved content to be published directly to your website's CMS without manual intervention.

**Command Palette**: A quick search feature (Ctrl+K or Cmd+K) that allows you to find content, clients, and pages without navigating through menus.

**Recurring Content Plan**: An automated schedule that generates content at regular intervals (daily, weekly, monthly) without requiring individual briefs for each piece.

**SEO (Search Engine Optimization)**: The practice of optimizing content to rank higher in search engine results, increasing organic traffic to your website.
