## Getting Started

### Accessing the Portal

Your agency will provide you with a unique portal URL and login credentials. The portal is accessible from any modern web browser on desktop, tablet, or mobile devices.

**To log in:**

1. Navigate to the portal URL provided by your agency (typically in the format `https://portal.youragency.com/client-portal`)
2. Click the **"Sign In"** button
3. Enter your credentials using your preferred authentication method (Google, email, or other options provided)
4. Once authenticated, you will be directed to your personalized dashboard

### First-Time Setup

Upon your first login, you will see a dashboard displaying all content created for your account. The interface is designed to be intuitive, with a clean layout that prioritizes content preview and easy navigation.

The portal uses your agency's branding, including their logo and color scheme, to provide a seamless white-labeled experience. All content you see is specific to your account and is not visible to other clients.

## Navigating the Portal

### Dashboard Overview

The main dashboard serves as your home base within the portal. It displays all blog posts and content pieces created for your account, organized in a grid or list format for easy browsing.

**Key elements of the dashboard:**

- **Content Cards**: Each content piece is displayed as a card showing the title, featured image, creation date, and current status
- **Status Indicators**: Visual badges indicate whether content is in draft, in progress, or approved status
- **Search and Filter**: Use the search bar at the top to quickly find specific content by title or keyword
- **Sorting Options**: Sort content by date, status, or title to organize your view

### Understanding the Interface

The portal interface is divided into several key areas:

**Header Section**: Located at the top of the page, the header displays your agency's logo and provides access to your user profile and logout options.

**Content Grid**: The main area displays all your content in an easy-to-scan format. Each content card provides a quick overview including the featured image, title, excerpt, and status badge.

**Content Details**: Clicking on any content card opens the full content view where you can read the complete blog post, view the AI-generated featured image, and take action.

## Reviewing Content

### Opening Content for Review

To review a piece of content, simply click on the content card from your dashboard. This opens the full content view with several tabs and sections.

### Preview Tab

The **Preview** tab is your primary view for reviewing content. This tab displays the content exactly as it would appear on your website, complete with:

- **Featured Image**: The AI-generated image that will accompany the blog post
- **Title**: The headline of the blog post
- **Full Content**: The complete article text with proper formatting, headings, and paragraph structure
- **Metadata**: Information about word count, reading time, and SEO optimization

The preview is designed to give you a realistic representation of how the content will look when published. All formatting, including bold text, headings, lists, and links, is preserved in the preview.

### Edit Tab

If you need to make changes to the content, switch to the **Edit** tab. This tab provides a rich text editor where you can:

- Modify the title or any text within the article
- Adjust formatting (bold, italic, headings, lists)
- Add or remove paragraphs
- Insert or modify links
- Make any other textual changes

**Important**: Changes made in the Edit tab are automatically saved when you click the **"Save Changes"** button. Always save your changes before navigating away from the page.

### Content Details Sidebar

On the right side of the content view, you will find the **Content Details** sidebar, which provides:

- **Status**: Current approval status of the content
- **Progress**: A visual progress bar showing completion percentage
- **Topic**: The original topic or brief that generated this content
- **Type**: Content type (typically "BLOG POST")
- **AI Model Info**: Details about the AI model used for generation
- **Token Usage**: Information about AI resource consumption (for informational purposes)

## Providing Feedback

### Adding Comments

The portal includes a robust commenting system that allows you to provide specific feedback on content. To add a comment:

1. Navigate to the **Collaboration** or **Comments** section within the content view
2. Type your feedback in the comment box
3. Click **"Submit Comment"** to send your feedback to your agency

Your comments are immediately visible to your agency team, who will receive notifications about your feedback. This creates a streamlined communication channel focused specifically on each piece of content.

### Types of Feedback

When providing feedback, consider being specific about:

- **Content Accuracy**: Point out any factual errors or information that needs verification
- **Tone and Voice**: Indicate if the tone doesn't match your brand voice
- **Structural Changes**: Suggest reorganization of sections or paragraphs
- **Additions or Deletions**: Request specific information to be added or removed
- **SEO Concerns**: Mention if certain keywords or phrases should be emphasized

The more specific your feedback, the faster your agency can make revisions and deliver content that perfectly matches your expectations.

### Requesting Revisions

If content requires significant changes, you can request a revision by:

1. Adding detailed comments explaining the needed changes
2. Changing the content status back to "In Progress" (if you have permission)
3. Contacting your agency directly through the portal's notification system

Your agency will receive an alert about the revision request and will prioritize making the necessary changes.

## Approving Content

### Approval Process

Once you have reviewed content and are satisfied with its quality, you can approve it for publication. The approval process is straightforward:

1. Review the content thoroughly in the Preview tab
2. Verify that all information is accurate and the tone is appropriate
3. Locate the **Status** dropdown in the Content Details sidebar
4. Change the status from "Draft" or "In Progress" to **"Approved"**
5. Click **"Save Changes"** to confirm the approval

### What Happens After Approval

When you approve content, several automated processes are triggered:

- **Notification**: Your agency receives an immediate notification that content has been approved
- **Publishing Queue**: The content moves into the publishing queue for scheduling or immediate publication
- **Analytics Tracking**: The system begins tracking performance metrics once the content is published
- **Email Confirmation**: You may receive an email confirmation of the approval (depending on your agency's settings)

### Conditional Approval

If you approve content with minor conditions (such as "approved pending one small change"), make sure to:

1. Add a comment clearly stating the condition
2. Mark the status as "Approved" if the change is minor
3. Or keep it as "In Progress" if the change is substantial

Clear communication ensures your agency understands exactly what is needed before publication.

## Viewing Analytics

### Performance Metrics

Once content is published, the portal tracks various performance metrics that help you understand how your content is performing. Access analytics by navigating to the **Analytics** section from the main menu.

**Key metrics include:**

- **Page Views**: Total number of times the content has been viewed
- **Engagement Rate**: Percentage of visitors who interacted with the content
- **Time on Page**: Average duration visitors spend reading the content
- **Social Shares**: Number of times content has been shared on social platforms
- **Conversion Tracking**: Actions taken by visitors after reading the content

### Content Performance Dashboard

The analytics dashboard provides visual representations of your content performance through charts and graphs. You can:

- View performance trends over time
- Compare different pieces of content
- Identify top-performing topics
- Track overall content strategy effectiveness

This data-driven approach helps you and your agency make informed decisions about future content topics and strategies.

## Submitting Content Briefs

### What is a Content Brief?

A content brief is a document that outlines your requirements for a new piece of content. It includes information such as the topic, target keywords, audience, desired tone, and any specific points you want covered.

### How to Submit a Brief

Your agency may provide you with a dedicated **Content Brief Form** accessible through the portal. To submit a brief:

1. Navigate to the **Submit Brief** or **New Content Request** section
2. Fill out the form with the following information:
   - **Topic**: The main subject of the content
   - **Target Keywords**: SEO keywords you want to rank for
   - **Audience**: Description of your target readers
   - **Tone**: Desired writing style (professional, casual, technical, etc.)
   - **Key Points**: Specific information or messages to include
   - **References**: Any source materials or competitor articles to reference
   - **Deadline**: When you need the content completed

3. Click **"Submit Brief"** to send your request to your agency

### Brief Submission Best Practices

To help your agency create the best possible content, provide as much detail as possible in your briefs:

- **Be Specific**: Instead of "write about marketing," specify "write about email marketing strategies for e-commerce businesses"
- **Include Examples**: Link to articles or content styles you admire
- **Define Success**: Explain what success looks like for this content (rankings, leads, awareness, etc.)
- **Provide Context**: Share any relevant background information about your business or industry

The more information you provide, the better your agency can tailor content to your exact needs.

## Understanding Content Status

### Status Definitions

Content in the portal moves through several status stages:

**Draft**: Content has been generated but is not yet ready for your review. Your agency is still refining and optimizing the piece.

**In Progress**: Content is ready for your review and feedback. This status indicates that the content is in an active revision cycle.

**Approved**: You have reviewed and approved the content for publication. It is now ready to be published to your website.

### Status Workflow

The typical content workflow follows this pattern:

1. Your agency receives a content brief or creates content based on your recurring plan
2. AI generates the initial draft (status: **Draft**)
3. Your agency reviews and refines the content
4. Content status changes to **In Progress** and you are notified to review
5. You review the content and provide feedback or approval
6. If feedback is provided, the agency makes revisions and the content remains **In Progress**
7. Once you approve, status changes to **Approved**
8. Your agency publishes the content to your website

Understanding this workflow helps you know what to expect at each stage and when action is required from you.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How often should I check the portal?

Your agency will notify you via email when new content is ready for review. We recommend checking the portal at least once per week to stay on top of your content pipeline. If you have an active content schedule, you may want to check more frequently.

### Can I edit content directly in the portal?

Yes, the portal includes a rich text editor that allows you to make changes directly to the content. Any changes you make are saved and will be reflected in the final published version. However, for major revisions, we recommend adding comments with your feedback so your agency can make the changes while maintaining proper formatting and SEO optimization.

### What if I need content urgently?

If you have an urgent content need, contact your agency directly through the portal's notification system or via your usual communication channels. While the portal streamlines the review process, urgent requests may require direct communication to ensure proper prioritization.

### How do I know when content has been published?

Your agency will typically notify you when content has been published to your website. Additionally, you can check the analytics section of the portal, which will begin showing performance data once content is live.

### Can I download content for offline review?

Yes, the portal includes export functionality. You can download content in multiple formats including Word, PDF, and Markdown. Look for the **"Export"** button in the content view and select your preferred format.

### What happens to content I don't approve?

If you don't approve content, it remains in "In Progress" status. Your agency will follow up with you to understand your concerns and make necessary revisions. Content is never published without your explicit approval.

### Can multiple people from my team access the portal?

This depends on your agency's setup. Contact your agency to discuss adding additional team members to your account if you need multiple people to review and approve content.

### Is my content secure?

Yes, the portal uses industry-standard security measures including encrypted connections (HTTPS), secure authentication, and role-based access controls. Your content is only visible to you and your agency team.

## Getting Support

### Contacting Your Agency

If you encounter any issues or have questions not covered in this manual, your agency is your first point of contact. You can reach them through:

- **Portal Notifications**: Use the built-in notification system to send messages directly to your agency team
- **Email**: Contact your agency using the email address they provided during onboarding
- **Phone**: Call your agency's support line for urgent issues

### Technical Issues

If you experience technical difficulties with the portal itself (such as login problems, page loading issues, or display errors):

1. Try clearing your browser cache and cookies
2. Ensure you're using a supported browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge - latest versions)
3. Check your internet connection
4. Try accessing the portal from a different device

If problems persist, contact your agency with details about the issue, including:

- What you were trying to do when the error occurred
- Any error messages you received
- The browser and device you're using
- Screenshots of the issue (if possible)

### Feedback on the Portal

Your agency values your feedback on the portal experience. If you have suggestions for improvements or features you'd like to see added, please share them with your agency team. The portal is continuously evolving to better serve your needs.
